    Help and Instructions

    Generate a single QR code

    Type or paste a link or text, choose URL or Plain-text mode, and click Generate QR Code (or press Enter). Add an optional caption and choose its size and colour; the preview updates instantly.

    Generate many QR codes

    Open the Bulk QR Codes tab, paste one entry per line (up to 100) and click Generate All QR Codes. Edit captions per card, or use the common caption. Untick cards you do not want exported.

    Download and print

    Download PNG saves a high-resolution image with the caption. Download PDF saves an A4 page. For bulk, use Download All as ZIP (separate PNGs plus an index.csv) or Download Bulk PDF (one per page or 9 per page). Print QR Code prints only the QR code and caption.

    UPI payment QR codes

    Type a UPI ID such as name@bank (in URL mode it is detected automatically, or choose UPI ID mode). The QR code opens as a payment request in UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) and the payer enters the amount. Always test-scan and confirm the payee name before sharing.

    Scanning tips

    Keep the white margin around the code, print at least 2 cm wide, use high contrast, and test-scan with a phone before distributing.

    Privacy

    Nothing is uploaded. The file makes no network requests, has no analytics and stores nothing permanently. History disappears when you refresh the page.
